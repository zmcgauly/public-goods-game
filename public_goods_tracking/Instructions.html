Welcome, and thank you for participating in this research study.

Please place all electronic devices out of reach for the duration of the experiment. To ensure that all participants have the same experimental environment, conversation and other forms of communication are not permitted while the session is in progress. If you have a question at any time, please raise your hand. An experimenter will come to your workstation and answer your question privately.

This study investigates economic decision-making. Throughout the experiment, you will make decisions that determine your own earnings and can affect the earnings of other participants.

You have earned a show-up fee of $10. This payment is guaranteed. Your participation is voluntary, and you may withdraw from the experiment at any time without penalty. If you leave before the experiment has concluded, you will still receive the $10 show-up fee, but you will forfeit any additional earnings from decisions in the experiment.

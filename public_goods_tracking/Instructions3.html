In every round, each participant receives {{ C.ENDOWMENT }} balls.

You choose how many balls, from 0 to {{ C.ENDOWMENT }}, to contribute to the public good. You keep any balls you do not contribute.

After all members of your group make their decisions, the computer adds the group contributions together.

The total contribution is multiplied by {{ C.MULTIPLIER }} and then split equally among all {{ C.PLAYERS_PER_GROUP }} group members.

Your round payoff is calculated as:

{{ C.ENDOWMENT }} - your contribution + your share of the multiplied group contribution.

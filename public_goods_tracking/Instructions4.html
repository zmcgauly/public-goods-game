After each contribution decision, you will estimate what each of the other players in your group contributed.

For each player, you will distribute {{ C.BALLS_PER_ESTIMATE }} balls across possible contribution amounts from 0 to {{ C.ENDOWMENT }}.

Placing more balls on an amount means you think that amount is more likely. You must place all {{ C.BALLS_PER_ESTIMATE }} balls before continuing.

Each ball placed on the amount that the other player actually contributed earns {{ C.REWARD_PER_BALL }} additional ball.

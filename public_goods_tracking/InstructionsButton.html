Instructions Summary

You will be placed in groups of {{ C.PLAYERS_PER_GROUP }} participants. The experiment has {{ C.NUM_PERIODS }} periods, with two rounds in each period.

In each round, you receive {{ C.ENDOWMENT }} balls and choose how many to contribute to the public good. You keep any balls you do not contribute.

The group contribution is multiplied by {{ C.MULTIPLIER }} and split equally among the {{ C.PLAYERS_PER_GROUP }} group members.

Your round payoff is: {{ C.ENDOWMENT }} - your contribution + your share of the multiplied group contribution.

After contributing, you estimate the contributions of the other group members by distributing {{ C.BALLS_PER_ESTIMATE }} balls across the possible contribution amounts.

At the end, one period is randomly selected for the guessing payoff. You also answer self-identification questions and guess information about the players you encountered.

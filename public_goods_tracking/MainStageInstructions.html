In the main stage, you will decide how many of your 10 balls you want to contribute to a shared group account.

At the beginning of each round, you will be given 10 balls for that round. These 10 balls are only used for making your decision in that round. In the next round, you will receive 10 new balls.

You will be placed in a group of three players. You will be shown your group and reminded that you have 10 balls for the round. You must choose how many of those balls to contribute to the shared account by entering a number in the text entry box.

You may enter any whole number from 0 to 10. The number you enter is your contribution to the shared account for that round. Any balls you do not contribute are not added to the shared account. However, balls that you do not contribute do not automatically become payment for you.

After all three players in your group make their contribution decisions, the contributions from all three players are added together. This total is then multiplied by 1.6. The multiplied amount is divided equally among all three players in the group, regardless of how much each person contributed.

The shared account calculation is part of the decision-making task, but it will not be paid to you. Your payment outside of the show-up fee will come only from the elicitation stage in the randomly selected round.

The main-stage group account payoff value is calculated as:

\[ \begin{aligned} \text{main-stage group account payoff value} &= (\text{your endowment} - \text{your contribution to the group account}) \\ &\quad + 1.6 \times \left(\frac{\text{all group contributions}}{3}\right) \end{aligned} \]

Using the example below, suppose the three players contribute 2, 5, and 8 balls. All contributions together equal 15 balls. If you contributed 2 balls, your main-stage group account payoff value would be:

\[ \begin{aligned} \text{main-stage group account payoff value} &= (10 - 2) + 1.6 \times \left(\frac{15}{3}\right) \\ &= 8 + 8 \\ &= 16 \end{aligned} \]

So, in this example, the calculated main-stage group account payoff value for the player who contributed 2 balls would be 16.

For example, suppose the three players contribute 2, 5, and 8 balls. The total contribution to the shared account would be 15 balls. That total would be multiplied by 1.6, creating 24 balls. The 24 balls would then be divided equally among the three players, so each player would receive 8 balls from the shared account.

In this example:

  • The player who contributed 2 balls contributed the least to the shared account.
  • The player who contributed 5 balls made a middle contribution.
  • The player who contributed 8 balls contributed the most to the shared account.
  • All three players would receive the same 8-ball share from the shared account because the multiplied total is divided equally.

Your decision affects the size of the shared account and therefore affects both your own outcome and the outcomes of the other players in your group. Higher contributions increase the total amount in the shared account, which increases the amount divided among all group members. Lower contributions reduce the total amount generated by the shared account.

Once you enter your contribution and click Next, your decision for that round is submitted. After all players have submitted their decisions, the experiment will move to the next stage.

For this experiment, you will participate in ten rounds. Each round can be divided into two stages: the main stage and the elicitation stage. After the completion of the ten rounds, you will be asked to complete a short survey. Once both the ten rounds and the survey are completed, one of the ten rounds you played will be randomly selected for payment. The earnings from the round selected to pay out will be reported to you and the experiment will conclude.

Before the rounds begin, you will be asked to confirm that the picture on the screen is you and enter the player number you were given upon entry. If you do not see yourself on the screen when prompted, please raise your hand and wait for an experimenter. If you do not have or do not recall your player number, raise your hand and someone will come over to give it to you.

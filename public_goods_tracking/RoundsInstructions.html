For this experiment, you will participate in ten rounds. Each round can be divided into two stages: the main stage and the elicitation stage. The ten rounds are grouped into five periods. Each period contains two rounds with the same group. After the completion of the ten rounds, you will be asked to complete a short survey. Once both the ten rounds and the survey are completed, one of the five periods you played will be randomly selected for payment. The earnings from the period selected to pay out will be reported to you and the experiment will conclude.

Before the rounds begin, you will be asked to confirm that the picture on the screen is you and enter the player number shown on your participant ID card. If you do not see yourself on the screen when prompted, please raise your hand and wait for an experimenter. If you do not have or do not recall your participant ID card number, raise your hand and someone will come over to help you.
